Improving bar following

Exploratory causal MIDI experiment. All methods receive a known unfolded chord plan, starting position/time, and approximate catalog BPM. Piano MIDI is absent. These are experimental implementations, not published full-system reproductions. Independent MIDI/audio/annotation time-origin verification remains outstanding.

Every table averages per-song metrics equally and separates async from sync. A better exact-bar percentage may coexist with larger catastrophic errors; inspect within-two-beat accuracy and absolute error too. Beat reference positions are interpolated inside annotated bars.

Setting selection and consistency

loo_global selects a setting on all other songs, weighting the two protocol means equally. loo_protocol uses other songs with the same known recording protocol. Neither chooses the best setting using the evaluated song's labels. These are retrospective internal checks, not an untouched test set; protocol-conditioned selection is only applicable when recording conditions are known.
