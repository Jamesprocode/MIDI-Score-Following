Exploratory causal MIDI experiment. All methods receive a known unfolded chord plan, starting position/time, and approximate catalog BPM. Piano MIDI is absent. Every table averages per-song metrics equally and separates async from sync. A better exact-bar percentage may coexist with larger catastrophic errors; inspect within-two-beat accuracy and absolute error too. Beat reference positions are interpolated inside annotated bars.
